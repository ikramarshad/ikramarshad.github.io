Apna Khata — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · applies to Apna Khata 2.2.2 and later

Apna Khata ("the app") is a personal and small-business bookkeeping app published by Ikram Arshad ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles. Questions: cybertech432@gmail.com. You can also find this policy and the contact email in the app under Settings › About.

1. Your bookkeeping data stays on your phone

Everything you enter — names, amounts, categories, ledgers, notes, receipts, your logo and backups — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device, or in a backup file at a location you choose. We do not have servers, we do not receive this data, and the app never uploads it. There is no account or login. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app.

App lock uses your phone's own screen lock or fingerprint through Android; the app never sees your PIN or fingerprint.

Optional permissions:

  • Notifications — reminders you set up (udhaar due dates, recurring entries, balance checks).
  • Alarms & reminders — to ring ledger reminders at the exact time you choose.

Files you share into the app (receipt screenshots, PDFs, text) are copied into its private storage and used only to fill in the entry.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). These are banner ads, full-screen ads shown after some entries you save, and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch for 10 minutes without ads. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads),
  • IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived),
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and ad interactions (views, taps), plus diagnostic data such as crash logs of the ad SDK.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit. The ad SDK never receives your bookkeeping records. How Google uses it: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or a region with similar laws (including certain US states), the app asks for your choice through Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time under Settings › Privacy & ad choices (ترتیب / Tarteeb in the Urdu app). Ads shown are limited to a "PG" content rating.

3. Google Play features

The app also uses Google Play's in-app review and in-app update features, which let Google Play show a rating card or offer a newer version; Google handles any rating you give.

4. Children

Apna Khata is a finance tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

5. Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your phone, you control it completely: delete entries in the app, or uninstall the app / use Android Settings › Apps › Apna Khata › Clear storage to remove everything. Backup files you saved elsewhere must be deleted by you. Advertising data held by Google can be managed at https://myadcenter.google.com.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
