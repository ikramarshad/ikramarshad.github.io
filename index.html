Apna Khata

Expense & Udhaar · for Android

Apna Khata is a simple, private daily khata (ledger) for your money. Write down every expense, income and udhaar in a few taps, and always know how much cash is in your pocket and how much is in your bank or wallet.

  • Add an entry in seconds, with a built-in calculator in the amount box.
  • Separate balances for cash in hand and online (bank / wallet).
  • Udhaar ledgers with reminders, reports and backups.
  • Works offline. No account, no sign-up. Your records stay on your phone.
