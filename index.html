Inside the app

Five looks, one simple khata

Everything you need

Simple enough for every day

Add in seconds

Tap Expense, type the amount, save. The amount box is a calculator, so 120+45 just works.

Cash and online

Separate balances for cash in hand and bank or wallet, with transfers for ATM withdrawals and deposits.

Udhaar ledgers

A running khata for each person: "I gave" and "I took". Settle up in one tap and share a statement on WhatsApp.

Reports and budgets

Daily, monthly and custom reports, a 6-month chart, and budgets per category. Export to Excel or PDF.

Your language

English, Roman Urdu or Urdu in Nastaliq, right to left. Pick your country and the currency is set for you.

Backup and restore

Save a backup to any folder you choose, and restore it on the very first screen of a new phone.

Private by design

Your records stay on your phone

Apna Khata has no servers and no account. We never see your entries.

  • Works fully offline
  • No sign-up, no login
  • Optional app lock with your phone's own screen lock
  • Backups go only to a folder you pick
